# Outstanding, from the walk round of 2026-09-19

## 9. An arm stops when its owner stands on what it was reaching for

It should give up on that one and go to something else rather than waiting. Wants
investigating more thoroughly than the first pass: work out where the wait actually
happens rather than patching the symptom.

## 11. The tank's arms are bolted to the wrong place

Put their position where the central circular part of the tank's turret projects onto the
ground, and their visual offset so that they stand at the bottom edge of the turret.

## 12. An arm should aim where its target will be, not where it is

While the player or the vehicle is moving, assume the current heading and speed hold, and
reach for the position the target will have reached by the time the arm can reach it.
