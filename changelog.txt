Version: 2.1.5
  Features:
    - The arms now carry out upgrade planner orders, swapping the thing standing there for what it is marked to become and carrying the old one back to you in the claw
    - An arm making a swap carries one thing at a time, so its claw is free to bring the old one home even when your pockets are full
    - Anything the replacement cannot hold is left on the ground and marked for deconstruction, the same as a construction robot leaves it, rather than appearing in your inventory or being lost when it is full
    - The arms now pay for quality work with items of that quality, whether they are raising a ghost or carrying out an upgrade
    - Mirroring carries across an upgrade
    - Both ends of an underground belt go up together, from whichever end an arm can reach, for the price of two, and both old ends come home in the one trip however small the claw is, rather than one being left on the floor to fetch again
    - Whatever was travelling in an upgraded tunnel stays in the tunnel rather than being turned out onto the ground
    - An arm now carries a cliff explosive out to a cliff you have marked for deconstruction, at the cost of one, the same as a construction robot spends
    - The arms now clear away what you mark with a deconstruction planner, entities and tiles alike, carrying it home in the claw
    - A thing with something in it is emptied a clawful at a time and taken up last, once it is empty, the way construction robots do it
  Changes:
    - An arm is fed whether or not it has anything to do, so one that has been standing about is ready the moment work arrives rather than spending its first ticks filling up
    - A character with something to build in reach and not the charge to build it wears the game's own low power mark, so an armour that has run flat says so rather than looking like nothing happening
    - Arms on a locomotive build out of the cargo wagons in its train, since a locomotive has no hold of its own
    - The equipment is one tile wide and a tile taller with every tier: one by two, three, four and five as they go up, so a modular armour holds one of any of them
    - Arms on a spidertron are bolted where its legs meet its body, one to a leg, and stay where they are when its torso turns; a ninth and beyond ride round the body as before
    - An arm goes for whatever it can reach soonest rather than whatever is nearest, counting the turn as well as the reach, which clears a scattered yard about half again as quickly
    - The toolbar button greys out when you are wearing no arm, the way the exoskeleton's does
    - An arm is built facing what it is about to reach for, to the nearest sixteenth of a turn, so a reach costs the same whichever way the thing lies: reaching one tile took between 5 and 17 ticks depending on the bearing and now takes between 6 and 10, and reaching five tiles behind its owner took three times what it took in front
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed the game ending when a player wearing arms climbed into a locomotive, which cannot take the slowdown sticker the arms put on their wearer
    - A claw with room left in its hand works a round of pickups, carrying its load from one thing to the next without coming home between them, and visits every one of them: nothing is taken up that the claw did not travel to
    - A claw being put away now fades where the arm is worn and moves with its owner, rather than off to one side and where they used to be standing
    - Your arms are offered work shortest first, so a long arm no longer takes the ghost under a short one's nose while work only it can reach waits
    - Fixed an arm coming out of your back and never setting off for something marked for deconstruction
    - Fixed losing what the claw had just been handed when the arms were switched off or taken away
    - Fixed losing what an arm was carrying when it was put away and your pockets had no room for it, which now goes on the floor beside you
    - Fixed losing an item when a claw was handed more than its hand could hold
    - An arm switched off part way through a reach now swings home first, hands back what it was carrying and then folds away, rather than being taken away where it stood
    - Fixed the game failing to load alongside Enhanced Shadows
    - An arm now casts the base game's own hand shadow, rather than a silhouette of the claw itself
    - An arm whose armour runs flat part way through a reach is now put away, handing back what it was carrying, rather than hanging at full stretch until the swing gives up on it
    - Fixed an arm coming out and standing there doing nothing when what it was sent to take up had something standing beside it, which is what a line of belts marked with the planner is
    - Fixed a claw sent to pick something up off the floor beside a full chest taking the chest's contents instead, handful after handful, while what it was sent for lay there
    - Fixed a claw settling on something marked and never taking it while its owner was moving, which stopped an arm picking anything up as you walked past it and stopped a vehicle's arms taking what lay under the hull
    - An arm no longer sets off for something it has nowhere to put: with full pockets it stays in rather than fetching one thing and standing there holding it, and it starts again the moment there is room
    - Fixed a claw working a round of several getting stuck on one of them for good, turning to a fresh target every tick without ever delivering or coming home
    - A claw switched off mid reach now comes all the way in before the arm folds away, rather than being taken away two thirds of a tile out with the item winking out in mid air
    - Switching the arms off and straight back on no longer puts a second arm out beside the one still swinging home, with a second item out of your pockets to go with it
    - A vehicle's arms are bolted inboard of its running gear and drawn up on the body, rather than out on the tracks or hanging off the bottom of the hull
    - A bulk claw carrying a round of ghosts now crosses straight from one to the next, rather than swinging all the way home and out again between each, which cost it a whole journey a ghost and left it looking as though it had given up on the one it had just turned to

Version: 2.1.3
Date: 2026-09-10
  Info:
    - Added a thumbnail, shown beside the mod in the mod manager and on the portal
  Features:
    - The arm sits on whichever side of you is your back, and shrinks away a second after it runs out of work
    - An inserter arm now reaches out of your back to each ghost, carries the item there in its claw and puts it down, then comes back for the next one
    - Wearing more than one of the equipment gives you an arm for each of them, all building at once
    - Added three further tiers of the equipment, reaching three, four and five tiles, with red, blue and green arms
    - Better arms slow you less: half as much at the second tier, and not at all at the third and fourth
    - Added a setting for what an arm does when it brings something back and your inventory is full: drop it on the ground, or hold it in the claw and wait until there is room
    - Tiers can be mixed, and each arm keeps its own reach and its own pace
    - Researching inserter capacity lets an arm carry more than one thing at a time, and it then turns from ghost to ghost rather than going home between each
    - An arm carries exactly what the inserter it is made from carries, so the fourth tier's bulk claw goes much further on that research than the others do
    - The arm gives up if you walk out of reach partway through, and carries the item back rather than losing it
    - And turns straight to another ghost that takes the same item instead of making a trip home for an identical one
    - The character now leans into the work, slowing over a second rather than dropping to their slowest at once
    - And comes back up to speed gradually once there is nothing left to build, rather than all at once
  Changes:
    - The arm reaches two tiles rather than four
    - How often an arm builds is now however fast its claw swings, rather than a fixed two a second: a better arm is a quicker one, and things close by go up faster than things at arm's length
    - The arm draws power from your armour as it swings and while it idles, at the rate the inserter it is made from draws, rather than a fixed amount per delivery
    - An arm waits for a full charge before setting off, and turns for home rather than starting a delivery it cannot pay for
    - Each piece of equipment holds the charge for its own arm, the way an armour holds charge for night vision, rather than the arm drawing straight from your batteries
    - You walk half as slowly while the first tier is working, and reach that speed twice as quickly
    - The equipment tooltip no longer claims a movement bonus of nothing and a consumption of one watt; what each tier really draws is in its description
    - The equipment is cheaper to make: an inserter and a circuit, rather than steel, gears and four long handed inserters
    - Researching it needs only automation science
    - Each higher tier is built out of the tier below it, a circuit and a steel plate
    - The arm is yellow, and each higher tier takes the colour of the inserter its arm comes from
    - Researching each tier now also needs whatever unlocks the inserter it is built out of, found in the technology tree rather than assumed, so it still holds if a mod moves them
    - The slowdown no longer fights other mods that change how fast you walk, and both effects now apply together
    - An arm takes what it needs out of your inventory when it sets off rather than when it arrives, and gives it back if it comes home without having built anything
    - An arm sent for something that takes more than its claw can hold, such as a curved rail, takes all of it with it
    - The third and fourth tier arms turn more slowly, so a claw no longer snaps round in an instant while its reach crawls
    - An arm waits for more charge before setting off, so an armour that cannot spare it keeps its arm at home rather than stranding it partway
  Bugfixes:
    - Fixed the arm twitching in place instead of building, when a ghost sat just outside its reach or under your feet
    - Fixed being left slowed for good if anything interrupted a build, and gave the speed back to anyone a previous version had left that way
    - Fixed an item being left on the ground beside a ghost instead of built into it, most often a wall or a solar panel
    - Fixed your speed surging back towards normal for a moment between one delivery and the next
